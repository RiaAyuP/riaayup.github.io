Ria Ayu Pramudita earned her Doctor of Engineering in Chemical Engineering from the Tokyo Institute of Technology, where her research — carried out in the Motokura–Manaka Laboratory — centered on catalysts that convert carbon dioxide into valuable chemical products using silicon-based reducing agents.

A mother with a D.Eng. in Chemical Engineering, now studying AI to bring it into her own field.

Her path has moved between the bench and the boardroom: from reporting on campus research at Institut Teknologi Bandung, to the Management Trainee program at Mitsui & Co. Indonesia, to post-doctoral catalysis research back in Tokyo. Today she is turning that same rigor toward AI-assisted software — building React and Supabase-powered tools that make chemical engineering education more accessible, in both Indonesian and English.
