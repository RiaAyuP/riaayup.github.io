About

Hi, I'm Sana.

I spent the first half of my career building products, and the second half realizing most "AI strategy" is really just good judgment about people, process, and timing — with the technology as the easy part.

Now I work with founders and leadership teams who know AI matters but aren't sure where to start, what to trust, or what to ignore. My job is to make that decision simple, honest, and specific to them — not a slide deck of trends.
